2. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ANTROR handles information in connection with ANTROR Code. The short version: the Product has no backend, and we collect nothing.

2.1 What stays on your device

  • API keys — stored only in your browser’s localStorage. They are sent directly from your browser to the Provider you chose. They are never transmitted to ANTROR.
  • Projects, files, chat history, and checkpoints — all stored locally in your browser. Clearing your browser data deletes them permanently.

2.2 What third parties see

When you send a prompt, your browser sends a request directly to your chosen Provider (for example Z.ai, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Groq, OpenRouter, or your local Ollama). That request includes your prompt, your API key, and — for context — the current contents of your project files. It is processed under that Provider’s own privacy policy, not ours. Do not paste secrets into projects or prompts if that concerns you.

2.3 What ANTROR collects

  • No analytics, no telemetry, no tracking cookies, no account, no server logs — because there is no ANTROR server involved in your use of the Product.
  • If you contact us for support, we keep the correspondence only to help you.

2.4 Deleting your data

Because everything is local, you are in full control. Use Settings → Workspace data → Erase everything inside the Product, or clear site data in your browser. There is nothing to request from us because we never had it.
